# 4.2 REVENUE STREAMS

## Overview
Agrivoltaic systems generate multiple revenue streams from electricity generation and agricultural production. This section details all potential income sources, pricing models, market access strategies, and realistic yield projections for French agrivoltaic projects.

---

## 4.2.1 Electricity Revenue

### Solar Energy Generation and Sales Models

#### A. Grid-Connected Systems (Most Common)

**Feed-in Tariff (FIT) Model** (Primary revenue mechanism in France):

France offers fixed-price feed-in tariffs for renewable electricity via the ARENH (Accès Régulé à l'Électricité Nucléaire Historique) mechanism and bilateral contracts.

**2024 France Feed-in Tariff Rates** (for solar PV):

| System Size | Technology | FIT Rate (€/kWh) | Contract Length | Eligibility |
|------------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **0-36 kW** | Rooftop | €0.30-0.40 | 20 years | Residential/small business |
| **36-100 kW** | Ground-mount | €0.18-0.25 | 20 years | Agriculture-compatible systems |
| **100-500 kW** | Ground-mount | €0.15-0.20 | 20 years | Commercial/agrivoltaic |
| **500 kW-5 MW** | Ground-mount | €0.12-0.18 | 15-20 years | Competitive bidding (sometimes) |
| **>5 MW** | Utility-scale | €0.10-0.15 | 15-20 years | Competitive auction required |

*Note: Rates declining 0.5-1% annually; 2024 rates shown; verify with local DSO (Enedis, EDF, SNCF Réseau)*

**Agrivoltaic Premium** (Under Discussion in France):
- Several regions (Occitanie, Nouvelle-Aquitaine) piloting +€0.02-0.05/kWh premium for certified agrivoltaic systems
- Certification requirements: Demonstrate agricultural viability, biodiversity standards
- Expected adoption: 2-3 years (estimated rollout 2026-2027)

**Annual Generation by System Size**:

| System Size | Annual Generation (MWh) | Annual FIT Revenue (€0.15/kWh avg) | Annual FIT Revenue (€0.20/kWh with premium) |
|------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 500 kW | 550-650 | €82,500-97,500 | €110,000-130,000 |
| 1 MW | 1,100-1,300 | €165,000-195,000 | €220,000-260,000 |
| 1.5 MW | 1,650-1,950 | €247,500-292,500 | €330,000-390,000 |
| 3 MW | 3,300-3,900 | €495,000-585,000 | €660,000-780,000 |

*Generation estimates based on 1,100-1,300 kWh/m²/year average solar resource across France (varies by region: 900-1,400 kWh/m²/year)*

---

**Power Purchase Agreements (PPA)** (Alternative/Complementary):

Direct contracts with private buyers (corporations, cooperatives, municipalities) at negotiated prices:

- **Corporate PPAs**: €0.08-0.15/kWh (below FIT but long-term volume guarantee)
- **Community Cooperatives**: €0.10-0.18/kWh (mission-aligned pricing)
- **Municipal Contracts**: €0.12-0.20/kWh (support local projects)
- **Contract Length**: 10-20 years

**Advantage vs. FIT**: Potentially higher price; volume certainty; environmental co-benefits
**Disadvantage**: Negotiation complexity; lower prices in competitive markets; volume risk if buyer defaults

---

#### B. Self-Consumption Model (Emerging)

For farms with significant daytime electricity demand (irrigation pumps, refrigeration, processing):

**Mechanisms**:
- Solar electricity used directly (not exported to grid)
- Excess exported to grid at standard rates
- Reduced grid electricity purchase cost (avoided retail rate ~€0.25-0.35/kWh)

**Economic Benefit**:
- Value of self-consumed electricity: €0.25-0.35/kWh (avoided retail cost) vs €0.15-0.20/kWh (FIT export)
- Example: 100 kW system, 50% self-consumption
  - 250 MWh exported/year @ €0.17/kWh = €42,500
  - 250 MWh self-consumed @ €0.30/kWh avoided cost = €75,000
  - **Total value: €117,500/year** (vs €85,000 all-FIT)

**Best For**:
- Vegetable operations with processing (refrigeration, freezing)
- Dairy with cooling/milking equipment
- Irrigation-intensive systems
- Mushroom production with climate control

---

#### C. Curtailment and Grid Services (Future)

Emerging revenue opportunity as grid operators need flexibility:

**Services**:
- Frequency regulation: €0.01-0.05/kWh
- Demand response: €0.02-0.08/kWh
- Peak shaving: €0.03-0.10/kWh
- Black start capability: €0.02-0.06/kWh (if battery included)

**Potential**: €5,000-30,000/year for 1-5 MW system (nascent market; limited current opportunity)

### Diversification Risk Mitigation

**Revenue Volatility by Enterprise** (coefficient of variation):

| Enterprise | Price Volatility | Production Volatility | Total Risk |
|-----------|----------------|--------------------|-----------|
| Electricity (FIT) | Very Low (fixed) | Very Low | **Very Low** |
| Sheep products | Moderate | Moderate | Moderate |
| Fresh vegetables | High (seasonal) | Moderate | High |
| Tree nuts (mature) | Moderate | Low | Low-Moderate |
| Processed foods | Low (controlled pricing) | Low | **Very Low** |
| Agritourism | High (weather, trends) | Moderate | High |

**Risk Reduction Strategy**:
1. Base income: Electricity (stable, fixed-price) = 40-60% of revenue
2. Secondary: Tree crops (mature, lower risk) = 10-20% of revenue
3. Tertiary: Processed/value-added (premium, stable demand) = 15-25%
4. Growth: Fresh products + agritourism (high-margin, higher risk) = 15-25%

**Portfolio Effect**: Combined coefficient of variation drops from High to Moderate through diversification

## 4.2.4 Pricing Strategies and Market Access

### Fresh Produce Pricing

**Price Hierarchy by Sales Channel** (€/kg lettuce example):

```
Wholesale/Supermarket:     €0.50-0.80/kg (low margin)
    ↓
Farmer aggregator/distributor: €0.80-1.00/kg
    ↓
Farm-gate/direct wholesale:  €1.00-1.20/kg
    ↓
Farm shop:                  €1.50-2.00/kg (moderate margin)
    ↓
Farmers' market:            €1.80-2.50/kg (good margin)
    ↓
Restaurant delivery/subscription: €1.50-2.20/kg
    ↓
Online/mail order:          €2.00-3.00/kg + shipping
    ↓
Value-added (prepared salads): €4-8/kg (high margin)
```

**Optimal Strategy**: Mix of channels
- 40% farmers' market/direct (highest margin)
- 35% farm shop + restaurant contracts (good margin)
- 25% wholesale (volume, reliability)
- **Blended average price: €1.40-1.80/kg** (vs €0.60-0.80 wholesale only)

### Market Development Timeline

**Year 1-2**: Market development phase
- Establish relationships (farm shop owners, restaurant chefs, market vendors)

- Limited product range, higher costs, lower prices
- **Pricing: €0.80-1.20/kg average**

**Year 3-5**: Growth phase
- Expand channels (multiple markets, restaurant contracts)
- Improve efficiency (lower per-unit production cost)
- Introduce value-added products
- Establish brand ("solar-grown" marketing)
- **Pricing: €1.20-1.80/kg average**

**Year 5+**: Maturity phase
- Integrated supply chains (subscription boxes, online ordering)
- Processing facility (pesto, pickled, dried products)
- Agritourism (farm shop, tours, events)
- Premium brand positioning
- **Pricing: €1.50-2.50/kg (products) + €3-8/kg (value-added)**
